I am an Italian fashion designer who graduated from a fashion academy in Italy
and later pursued specialised training in pattern making in Paris.
My background combines creative design with a strong technical understanding of garment development,
allowing me to approach fashion from both an artistic and a practical perspective.
Through my experience and training, I have developed technical skills in flat pattern cutting,
draping, garment construction, and digital pattern making using Modaris Expert CAD.
My approach is rooted in precision, attention to detail, and a hands-on understanding of the product,
from the initial concept to the final garment.
My passion for fashion lies particularly in prêt-à-porter and luxury, where creativity, craftsmanship, and technical excellence come together.
Through my professional experience, I have developed a deeper appreciation for the construction of a garment,
the quality of materials, and the attention to detail that define a finished piece.
I am drawn to the dialogue between design and technique,
and to the process of translating a creative vision into a well-crafted, thoughtfully developed product.
My work balances artistic sensitivity with precision and structure,
combining a curiosity for experimentation with a rigorous approach to making.
I am particularly interested in working closely with creative teams,
contributing to the development of collections and bringing ideas to life through pattern making and garment construction.
